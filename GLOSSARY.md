# Portfolio

The words used when deciding what this site shows, to whom, and how it looks.

## Readers

**Screener**:
An HR person or non-technical recruiter deciding in seconds whether to read on, mainly from the first screen and `/hiring/`.
_Avoid_: HR, recruiter (when the technical kind is meant too)

**Technical reviewer**:
An engineer or technical recruiter who opens case studies to judge method, evidence and code.
_Avoid_: tech recruiter, developer

## Work

**Flagship**:
One of the three projects shown first, each with a case study and a measured headline result.
_Avoid_: featured project, main project

**Project kind**:
What sort of thing a project is: Product, Open source, Research or Tool. Each kind has its own colour.
_Avoid_: type, category, tone

## Look

**Colour**:
Hue on surfaces, text and marks that carries a meaning (such as a project's kind), not decoration.
_Avoid_: visuals (when only colour is meant)

**Imagery**:
A picture of something real: a photo of Omkar, a screenshot of a product, or an illustration.
_Avoid_: visuals (when only imagery is meant), graphics

**Data graphic**:
A chart or diagram drawn from a measured result.
_Avoid_: visual, illustration
